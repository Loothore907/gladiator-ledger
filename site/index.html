Gladiator Ledger

Of these two models, which does this task better?

Ledger answers from head-to-head matches: two models play the same freshly generated worlds of a tool-using workflow, and each pair of attempts is judged. It makes a claim about one task, never a general ranking, and it names no winner when the evidence is thin or level. There is no leaderboard: for your own flow, the record of the closest tested workflow is a starting point, and a match on your task is the evidence.

3 Verdict and spec

Every verdict on this page rests on recorded matches: finished, sealed, and replayable here. The page reads them locally in this browser tab and needs no key. Live races run on a separate Gladiator runner and are open to the hackathon judges for now (Judge sign-in, at the top); a live result is shown beside the record and is never counted in a verdict.

Published scores are used only to suggest a fair opponent, never to rank or recommend. They come from Epoch AI, “Capabilities & benchmarking” (the Epoch Capabilities Index), epoch.ai/benchmarks, licensed CC BY 4.0.
