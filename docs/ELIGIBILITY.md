# Eligibility: what the organizers said

Before building, the author asked the hackathon organizers whether a new project that reads data from the author's
own earlier project could be entered.

- **Where:** the hackathon's public discussion forum on Devpost,
  [Eligibility question: new project that reads data from an earlier personal project](https://relate.devpost.com/forum_topics/45954-eligibility-question-new-project-that-reads-data-from-an-earlier-personal-project).
- **When:** asked and answered on 2026-11-22.
- **Who answered:** Ari (Neo4j), shown on the forum as a hackathon Manager.

## The reply, in full
> Thanks for checking before you get too far. Based on what you've described, this sounds like it could be an
> eligible entry: you'd build the new project and its Relate integration during the contest, including the loader,
> schema, queries, and recommendation workflow.
>
> You can reuse your earlier evaluation engine and its output data as long as you clearly disclose what existed
> before the contest and what you build during it. Please make Relate's role in the project clear in your
> submission. We're looking forward to seeing what you create!

## The two conditions, and where they are met
| Condition | Where it is met |
|---|---|
| Clearly disclose what existed before the contest and what was built during it | The "Before and during the contest" table in the [README](../README.md) and the full statement in [DISCLOSURE.md](DISCLOSURE.md) |
| Make Relate's role in the project clear | "Where Relate comes in" at the top of the [README](../README.md), "How and why Relate is used" further down, and [SCHEMA.md](SCHEMA.md) |

## Two things to read this with
- The reply says the entry "could be" eligible. It is guidance on the question as asked, not a ruling on the
  finished submission; the judges and the Official Rules decide.
- The question called Gladiator an "earlier" project. Checking the commit history afterwards showed that only the
  idea is earlier (October 2026): Gladiator's first code was committed on 2026-11-18, after the contest opened on
  Nov 11. It is disclosed as separate work all the same, with the dates, in [DISCLOSURE.md](DISCLOSURE.md).
